A Letter From Molly Denver, CO · 2026

Hello, I'm Molly.

Molly Engels is a J.D. candidate at the University of Denver Sturm College of Law graduating in December 2026, where her work centers on civil rights and criminal justice reform.

Her path to law took her through paralegal and legal assistant roles in Big Law and boutique firms in Washington, D.C., where she built deep experience supporting civil rights class actions and working with incarcerated individuals. She also serves as Assistant Director of Juvenile-In-Justice, documenting the lives of currently and formerly incarcerated individuals through photography and narrative.

In addition to her justice-oriented work, Molly has long been engaged with creatives—working alongside them in their story development, social media campaigns, branding implementation, event planning and design, and all else. Molly offers a wide range of skills—research, writing, interviewing, and careful craft—to creatives and mission-driven organizations who need a steady hand.

Yours,
M.E.

Molly grew up in Vermont, where winters were cold, summers meant picking blueberries, and early springs were filled with the love of real maple syrup. She's since called Washington, D.C., and Denver, Colorado home, and now shares her life with an English Bulldog named Basil and three cats — Eloise, Lemon, and Clementine.

Outside of work, she's drawn to making things with her hands, whether it is through jewelry making, silversmithing, floral design, or needlepoint, which are regular pastimes. Molly is especially energized by fellow creatives and mission-driven people who care as much about the why behind their work as the work itself.
